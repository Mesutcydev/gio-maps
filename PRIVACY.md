# Gio privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Gio is built to work offline. The developer does not collect, store or sell any personal data.

**No account, no tracking, no ads.** Gio has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party
tracking SDKs.

**Location.** Your location is used on your iPhone to show your position and guide you. It is not sent to the
developer.

**Voice.** When you hold the microphone, speech is turned into text on your iPhone. Recordings are not stored or
sent anywhere.

**Assistant.** The built-in assistant runs on your iPhone. Your questions and its answers stay on the device.

**Optional cloud models (off by default).** If you turn them on in Settings and agree to the consent screen,
your question and an approximate (rounded) location are sent for that request only:
- to Apple Private Cloud Compute, under Apple's privacy terms, or
- to the AI provider you choose, using your own API key, under that provider's terms.
API keys are stored only in your iPhone's Keychain. The developer never receives them.

**Map downloads.** Region packs are downloaded from GitHub (this repository). GitHub may log technical data such
as your IP address under its own privacy statement. Downloaded maps are stored only on your iPhone.

**Children.** Gio does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

**Contact.** Questions about privacy: [open an issue](https://github.com/Mesutcydev/gio-maps/issues).
